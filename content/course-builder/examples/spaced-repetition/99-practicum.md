# Practicum — Spaced Repetition, From First Principles

## Concepts under test

- `retrieval-practice` — the task must replace a re-reading habit with closed-book recall.
- `spacing-effect` — the task must produce a schedule whose gaps grow with success.

## Acceptance criteria

- A written review plan for real material the learner needs, covering at least three weeks.
- Every review step is a recall attempt (produce, then check), not a re-read.
- Gaps between reviews of the same item grow, and a failed recall shortens the next gap.
- After the first two reviews, the learner can show a record of which items were missed.

## Shape of a good task

Material the learner genuinely needs to keep for months, large enough that not everything can be
reviewed every day: a certification syllabus, a codebase's core modules, a vocabulary list.

## Anti-tasks

- Material needed only once, tomorrow — spacing cannot show its value.
- Writing a flashcard app — it exercises programming, not the two concepts.
- A schedule with fixed daily reviews — it ignores the spacing effect.
