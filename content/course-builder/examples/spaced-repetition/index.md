# Spaced Repetition, From First Principles

## About this course

A short example course for anyone who studies on their own and keeps losing what they learned.
It covers why memories fade and the two habits that slow the fade: testing yourself and spacing
the tests out. It does not cover flashcard software or scheduling algorithms in detail.

## Learning objectives

- O1: Explain what the forgetting curve predicts and what changes its slope.
- O2: Explain why retrieving an answer strengthens memory more than re-reading it.
- O3: Design a review schedule for new material with expanding gaps between reviews.

## Prerequisites

- None.

## Lessons

1. [The forgetting curve](01-the-forgetting-curve.md) — why memories decay, and how fast.
